                Next platinum: how hard?

                How this works

                Games in progress that have a platinum, easiest first. "Guide" is the platinum difficulty from trophy guides (PowerPyx, PSNProfiles, PlayStationTrophies; average when several rate it): up to 3.5 is Easy, up to 6 Medium, above that Hard. Without a guide rating, the level is estimated from PSN: "Plat" is the share of players who have the platinum, and missing trophies that under 10% of players have count against it. Tap a game for hours, missables and playthroughs.
